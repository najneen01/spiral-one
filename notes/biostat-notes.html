Bayesian Frameworks & Causal Inference

Advanced statistical methodologies for multi-layer omics integration, probabilistic perturbation cascades, and therapeutic target prioritization.

Topic 01 • Bayesian Inference

Hierarchical Propagation of Genetic Perturbations

Jointly modeling probabilistic cascades from GWAS variants to genes and core disease drivers without intermediate hard thresholds.

Methodological Foundation: Traditional target prioritization sequentially filters variants, introducing hard classification errors.

This hierarchical framework preserves posterior uncertainty across layers, evaluating $P(\text{Gene}|\text{Variant})$ and $P(\text{CoreGene}|\text{Gene})$ simultaneously.

Topic 02 • Multi-Omics Integration

Data Fusion Across eQTL, pQTL, and Perturb-Seq

Synthesizing heterogeneous molecular evidence into a unified latent variable framework for core-gene membership.

Data Harmonization: Combining regulatory networks, functional genomics, and causal gene-gene effects from Mendelian randomisation.

Resolves conflicting statistical signals while linking peripheral signals directly to omnigenic core disease networks.

Topic 03 • Target Prioritization

Estimating Causal and Actionable Probabilities

Quantifying $P(\text{Target}|\text{Disease})$ by merging rare pLOF burdens, common GWAS variants, and established drug targets.

Translational Endpoint: Translating statistical associations into a ranked catalogue of therapeutic candidates with rigorous confidence intervals.

Accelerates precision medicine pipelines by scoring both causality and therapeutic tractability.
