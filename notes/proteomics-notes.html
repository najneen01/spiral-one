Physics-Aware Deep Learning in Structural Models

Integrating thermodynamic constraints with equivariant graph networks to resolve 3D structural inaccuracies.

Key Concept: Standard data-driven models frequently fail physical validity checks when rotating molecules in space.

By incorporating strict vector invariant layers and electrostatic penalty metrics into PyTorch architectures, our models maintain atomic coordinate precision across complex protein folds.
