Scientific Insights & Modular Topics

Explore our comprehensive catalog of 8 structured research topics and analytical frameworks.

Topic 01

Physics-Aware Deep Learning

Integrating thermodynamic constraints with equivariant graph networks to resolve 3D structural inaccuracies.

Key Concept: Standard data-driven models frequently fail physical validity checks when rotating molecules in space.

By incorporating strict vector invariant layers and electrostatic penalty metrics into architectures, models maintain atomic coordinate precision.

Topic 02

CASP16 Benchmarking

Evaluating complex quaternary structure predictions against newly released experimental target datasets.

Evaluation Workflow: Unsolved target coordinates are assessed blindly.

Scoring utilizes Global Distance Test (GDT) metrics to benchmark hybrid architectures directly against homology pipelines.

Topic 03

Mass Spec & PPI Networks

Filtering high-throughput spectral libraries to reveal hidden allosteric target interaction sites.

Network Analysis: Processing raw mass spectrometry datasets to control false discovery rates (FDR).

Reconstructing dynamic interaction graphs uncovers actionable druggable interfaces critical for precision oncology.

Topic 04

Bayesian Perturb Cascades

Bayesian hierarchical frameworks to reconstruct perturbation cascades from GWAS variants to core disease genes.

Key Concept: Jointly modeling probabilistic cascades from GWAS variants to genes without hard thresholds.

Preserves posterior uncertainty across multi-omic layers, evaluating probabilities simultaneously.

Topic 05

Single-Cell Trajectory Inference

Single-cell RNA sequencing trajectory inference and spatial transcriptomics deconvolution algorithms.

Key Concept: Dynamic manifold learning for mapping cellular differentiation paths and lineage commitments.

Leveraging optimal transport theory to align multi-timepoint single-cell profiles and map tissue organization.

Topic 06

Multimodal Medical AI

Multimodal foundation models for prognostic modeling using electronic health records and medical imaging.

Key Concept: Transformer-based attention mechanisms applied to longitudinal patient timelines for early risk stratification.

Ensuring clinical interpretability via attention rollout metrics and rigorous out-of-distribution testing.

Topic 07

Target Prioritization Engines

Translating statistical associations into actionable catalogues of therapeutic candidates with confidence intervals.

Translational Endpoint: Merging rare pLOF burdens, common GWAS signals, and drug target databases.

Accelerates precision medicine pipelines by scoring both biological causality and structural tractability.

Topic 08

Genomic Data Harmonization

Synthesizing heterogeneous molecular evidence across eQTL, pQTL, and Perturb-Seq data repositories.

Data Fusion: Resolving conflicting statistical signals while linking peripheral signals directly to core networks.

Creates a unified latent variable framework for downstream functional validation experiments.
